The counterexample has a 4-byte buffer of zeros with an offset equal to the length, so strlen starts one past the end of the object. In real use, every call follows print_value or print_string_ptr, which have just written a NUL-terminated string into the printbuffer at the current offset after reserving space for the terminator. The cursor therefore always points inside the buffer at a terminated string. The harness leaves the content arbitrary and allows offset == length, which no caller produces.
