The counterexample sets which = 1 and leaves array->child->next (the second element) with prev = NULL. Line 34 then dereferences c->prev, which is NULL. In cJSON every element after the first has its prev pointing at its predecessor, so a real list cannot hold a second element whose prev is NULL. The counterexample therefore concerns the harness model, not a feasible caller input.
